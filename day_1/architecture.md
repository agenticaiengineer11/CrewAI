# Day 1 — CrewAI Architecture

## Project

AI Product Research & Analysis System

## User Input

The user provides a product and wants to know whether
it is worth selling in the USA.

## Agents

### 1. Product Research Agent

Role:
Product Research Specialist

Goal:
Research the product and collect relevant information.

Responsibility:
- Product characteristics
- Target customers
- Product demand
- Product use cases

Expected Output:
Product research report.


### 2. Competitor Analysis Agent

Role:
E-commerce Competitor Analyst

Goal:
Analyze competing products and sellers.

Responsibility:
- Identify competitors
- Analyze pricing
- Analyze positioning
- Identify competitor strengths
- Identify competitor weaknesses

Expected Output:
Competitor analysis report.


### 3. Market Analysis Agent

Role:
Market Research Analyst

Goal:
Evaluate the target market.

Responsibility:
- Market demand
- Target audience
- Market trends
- Market opportunities
- Market risks

Expected Output:
Market analysis report.


### 4. Profitability Agent

Role:
E-commerce Profitability Analyst

Goal:
Determine whether the product can be profitable.

Responsibility:
- Product cost
- Shipping cost
- Selling price
- Advertising cost
- Platform costs
- Estimated profit margin

Expected Output:
Profitability analysis.


### 5. Decision Agent

Role:
Business Decision Analyst

Goal:
Determine whether the product is worth selling.

Responsibility:
- Evaluate all research
- Compare opportunities and risks
- Calculate overall opportunity
- Give final recommendation

Expected Output:
Final product recommendation.
