# NovaHome Product Knowledge Base

## Company Overview

NovaHome is an online e-commerce company specializing in affordable smart-home and lifestyle products. The company primarily targets young adults, university students, first-time homeowners, and small families.

NovaHome focuses on products that are practical, easy to use, affordable, and suitable for everyday household needs.

## Pricing Strategy

NovaHome primarily targets customers looking for products below $50.

Products priced between $20 and $40 are considered the company's core price range.

Products above $50 require stronger differentiation and additional marketing justification.

The company generally avoids competing solely on the lowest price. Instead, it focuses on convenience, useful features, attractive design, and practical everyday benefits.

---

## Target Customer Profile

The primary NovaHome customer is between 18 and 35 years old.

Common characteristics include:

* Uses smartphones and computers regularly
* Shops online
* Values convenience
* Has a moderate disposable income
* Prefers practical products
* Responds well to short-form video demonstrations
* Often discovers products through social media
* Prefers products that solve a clear everyday problem

---

## Marketing Guidelines

NovaHome product advertisements should focus on the customer's problem rather than simply listing technical specifications.

For example, instead of:

"NovaGlow has three brightness modes."

A stronger marketing message is:

"Adjust your lighting instantly when switching from studying to relaxing."

Short-form video advertisements should demonstrate the product within the first few seconds.

Product demonstrations, before-and-after comparisons, and problem-solution videos are preferred over advertisements containing only static product images.

---

## Product Selection Rules

When evaluating a new product for NovaHome, researchers should consider:

1. Selling price should ideally be between $20 and $50.
2. The product should solve a recognizable customer problem.
3. The product should be easy to demonstrate visually.
4. The product should have potential for social-media marketing.
5. The product should not require complicated installation.
6. The product should have a clear target customer.
7. Products with strong differentiation are preferred over generic commodities.
8. Products with consistently low inventory should receive additional supply-chain investigation before aggressive advertising.

---

## Research Team Decision Framework

A product should be considered a strong candidate when it satisfies most of the following conditions:

* Price is within the target range.
* Customer problem is clear.
* Target audience is identifiable.
* Product can be demonstrated visually.
* Product has multiple useful features.
* Customer rating is above 4.3/5.
* Inventory is sufficient.
* Product has a clear marketing angle.

A product should receive additional investigation when:

* Inventory is low.
* Price is above $50.
* Customer rating is below 4.3/5.
* The product is difficult to demonstrate.
* The target customer is unclear.
* The product has weak differentiation.

---

## Example Business Scenario

The marketing manager wants to select one product for a new social-media advertising campaign.

The research team should compare the available products based on:

* Target audience
* Price
* Customer rating
* Inventory
* Problem solved
* Demonstration potential
* Marketing angle
* Overall product fit

The final recommendation should explain not only which product should be selected, but also why the product is a better candidate than the alternatives.
